# Third-party and proprietary material

The working copy deliberately excludes `client_local_English.dat`, translated DAT files, RAR archives, raw localization dumps, model binaries, GPU caches, and `datexport.dll`.

`datexport.dll` is a native x86 dependency used by the developer tool in the production folder. It must not be redistributed from this repository until its license and redistribution permission are documented.

`koniecdev/LotroKoniecDev` was reviewed as a public MIT-licensed reference. No
source file or binary was copied into this working copy. The source-digest,
stale-row and argument-masking ideas were reimplemented independently for the
Turkish catalog. If a future commit copies MIT code, its copyright notice and
the exact file attribution must be added here before publication.

LOTRO game files and extracted English strings may be proprietary or derivative material. Translation-memory and approved-translation files must remain private until a rights review authorizes publication.
